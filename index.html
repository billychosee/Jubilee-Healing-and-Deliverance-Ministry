Our History and Foundation

Jubilee Healing and Deliverance Ministry was founded in Zimbabwe by Apostle T. Deshu and Pastor Rejoice Deshu, out of a deep conviction and passion for the transformative power of Jesus Christ and the ministry of healing and deliverance.

The foundation of the ministry was built on the belief that freedom, healing and deliverance are the portion of every believer through Jesus Christ. We believe that no individual should remain bound by spiritual, physical, emotional or social challenges when the power of God is available to set them free.

While serving in Zimbabwe, the founders recognised that bondage was not only spiritual but could also manifest through social and educational challenges. One of the burdens placed upon their hearts was the yoke of illiteracy, particularly among underprivileged children who had limited access to education.

Driven by this conviction, they established a school for underprivileged children in Zimbabwe. The school was sponsored by the founders as part of their commitment to making a meaningful difference in the lives of children and their communities. This initiative reflected their belief that ministry goes beyond the pulpit; it must also touch lives, restore hope and create opportunities for a better future.

Our Journey to the United States

In 2019, Pastor Rejoice Deshu relocated to the United States, followed by Apostle T. Deshu in 2022.

Although geographical circumstances brought a transition from Zimbabwe to the United States, the vision and passion that gave birth to the ministry remained alive. The desire to see people experience the power of God through healing, deliverance, prayer and the ministry of the Word continued to burn in their hearts.

The relocation did not mark the end of the vision; rather, it presented an opportunity for the ministry to continue its assignment in a new environment.

The Continuation of the Vision

With a renewed commitment to the original vision, Jubilee Healing and Deliverance Ministry has begun monthly Healing and Deliverance Services in the United States.

These services are established with a heart to minister to individuals who are seeking divine intervention, spiritual freedom, healing, restoration and breakthrough. They provide an opportunity for people from different backgrounds and walks of life to come together in prayer, worship and the Word of God.

Our desire is to create an environment where individuals can encounter Jesus Christ, receive prayer and experience the transforming power of the Holy Spirit.

We remain committed to the belief that the Gospel must address the whole person. Just as our work in Zimbabwe extended to the educational needs of underprivileged children, we desire to continue making a positive impact in people's lives spiritually and socially.

Our Vision and Commitment

Jubilee Healing and Deliverance Ministry exists to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ and to see lives transformed through His healing and delivering power.

We believe in:

  • Freedom

    Breaking spiritual bondages and bringing people into the liberty found in Christ.

  • Healing

    Trusting God for physical, emotional and spiritual restoration.

  • Deliverance

    Ministering to those seeking freedom from spiritual oppression and bondage.

  • Restoration

    Helping individuals rediscover hope, purpose and their identity in Christ.

  • Community Impact

    Extending God's love beyond the church through practical initiatives that uplift and empower communities.

Our Declaration: Freedom, Healing and Deliverance is My Portion!

This is more than a slogan; it is the declaration that carries the heart of our ministry. We believe that Jesus Christ came to set captives free, heal the brokenhearted and restore those who are in need of His saving power.

Our journey began in Zimbabwe, but the vision extends beyond geographical boundaries. We are committed to continuing the assignment God has placed in our hands, reaching individuals, families and communities with the love, power and saving grace of Jesus Christ.
